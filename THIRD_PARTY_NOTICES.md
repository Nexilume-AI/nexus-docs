# Third-party components and assets

The website uses Docusaurus, React, Mermaid, the local search plugin and their transitive npm dependencies. Exact versions, registry origins and integrity hashes are recorded in package-lock.json. Their upstream licenses remain applicable; no third-party dependency source is vendored in this repository.

The three SVG assets under static/img are source illustrations (favicon and two schematic LuCI panels), not captures of live customer accounts. Downloads are the OpenWrt diagnostics script, SDK diagnostics script and the authored document-agent example. The SDK package itself is installed separately under its own Nexus Community License 1.0. Preserve attribution when replacing assets or adding third-party content.

See SECURITY.md for the remaining image-size advisories and the build-time mitigation.
